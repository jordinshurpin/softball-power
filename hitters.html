Explore Division I Hitters

Use this interactive page to search, filter, and inspect 2,175 individual NCAA Division I hitters from the 2022 season (qualified with at least 100 plate appearances).

Interactive Power vs. On-Base Scatter

Hover over any point to view the player’s name, school, home runs, and slash line (BA / OBP / SLG). You can also zoom into clusters of hitters or isolate specific home run tiers.
